Detailed Description

template<class Impl>
struct beman::fingertree::Traversable< Impl >

CRTP base for Traversable instances. Impl must provide traverse(applicative, f, container) and declare element_type. All other operations (transpose, for_each, traverse_with, transpose_with) are derived.
